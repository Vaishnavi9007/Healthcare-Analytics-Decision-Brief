Healthcare Analytics Decision Brief

Can a weekly medication-review queue improve timely pharmacy review?

Leadership discussion · Proposed operational pilot

SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — not real patient or pharmacy findings

Evidence and guardrails

Three views. Carefully bounded claims.

  • Patient cohort: age bands and aggregate risk flags
  • Pharmacy: fills and refill-gap examples
  • Safety: reported event counts and seriousness
  • Dispensing is not ingestion; report counts are not incidence or causation.

Replace the example CSVs with assigned, approved datasets.

Decision

Validate first. Pilot with governance.

  • Measure data completeness and review within seven days
  • Track adjudicated actionable reviews and refill-gap trends
  • Review missingness, subgroup coverage, privacy, and workload
  • Require clinical and privacy oversight before any real workflow.
